Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Campaign Tool ("the application") is a personal, non-commercial tool operated by an individual (the "owner"). This policy explains what data the application accesses and how it is handled.

Who this applies to

The application is used solely by its owner. It does not provide a service to third parties and has no other users.

What Google data is accessed

With the owner's consent, the application reads and writes the Google Sheets and Google Drive files that the owner uses to run and record email campaigns. It does not access any other Google data.

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to run the owner's email campaigns and keep their record. It is stored in the owner's own Google account. The data is not sold, shared, or disclosed to any third party, and is not used for advertising or profiling.

Consent and revocation

The owner can revoke the application's access at any time from their Google Account settings (Security, Third-party access).
