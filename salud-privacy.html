Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Salud ("the application") is a personal, non-commercial tool operated by an individual (the "owner"). This policy explains what data the application accesses and how it is handled.

Who this applies to

The application is used solely by its owner. It does not provide a service to third parties and has no other users.

What Google data is accessed

With the owner's consent, the application reads, through the Google Health API and with read-only access, the owner's own daily steps and distance. It does not access any other Google data, and it never writes to or modifies the owner's health data.

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to keep the owner's personal health record. The daily totals are stored in storage controlled by the owner (a private repository and the owner's own Google account). The data is not sold, shared, or disclosed to any third party, and is not used for advertising or profiling.

Consent and revocation

The owner can revoke the application's access at any time from their Google Account settings (Security, Third-party access).
