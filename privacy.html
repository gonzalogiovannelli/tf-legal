Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 July 2026

Tracker Financiero ("the application") is a personal, non-commercial tool operated by an individual (the "owner") to consolidate and analyse the owner's own bank transactions. This policy explains what data the application accesses and how it is handled.

Who this applies to

The application is used solely by its owner to access the owner's own bank accounts. It does not provide a service to any third parties and has no other users.

What data is accessed

  • Account details (account name, IBAN, currency).
  • Account balances.
  • Transaction data (date, amount, currency, description, merchant, references).

Data is accessed read-only through licensed open banking (PSD2) providers, with the owner's explicit consent granted at their bank. The application never initiates payments and cannot move funds.

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to consolidate, categorise and analyse the owner's finances for the owner's private use. Processed data is stored in the owner's own private Google Sheet. The data is not sold, shared, or disclosed to any third party, and is not used for advertising or profiling.

Consent and revocation

Access is granted via the owner's bank under PSD2 and expires periodically (approximately every 90 days). The owner can revoke access at any time from their bank or from the open banking provider.
